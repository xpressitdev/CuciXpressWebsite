## 1. The vision (what we're building)

cucixpress.com becomes the **unified digital platform** for Cuci Xpress, serving 5 surfaces from one codebase:

| Surface | URL | Audience | Auth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing | `cucixpress.com/` | Anyone | None |
| Live Queue | `cucixpress.com/queue` | Anyone | None |
| Customer Dashboard | `cucixpress.com/account` | Members | Google + WhatsApp OTP |
| POS | `cucixpress.com/pos` (staff subdomain or path) | Lane staff | Branch ID + password |
| CRM | `cucixpress.com/admin` | Owner + GM | Username + password + 2FA |

**Core principle:** One codebase. One database. One design system. Multiple surfaces.

`cuci-xpress.com` (LiveQue) is retired in Month 2-3 with permanent redirect to `cucixpress.com/queue`.

## 7. Customer model (the strategic core)

### Two equally important customer paths

```
WALK-IN PATH (~99% of revenue today)
─────────────────────────────────────
Drive in → Staff enters plate (or LPR scans it) → System recognizes vehicle
→ Auto-applies subscription if active → Wash → Drive out

What customer needs from app: nothing
What we need to provide: frictionless lane experience, optional digital receipt

ONLINE COMMUNITY PATH (~1% of customers today, growing)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Browse cucixpress.com → See branch queues, pricing, FAQs
→ Sign up for membership → Pocket Pay checkout → First wash uses QR code
→ Subsequent washes recognized by plate → Manage subscription online

What customer needs from app: account, history, subscription management
What we need to provide: smooth signup, easy management, clear value
```

### The bridge: License Plate Recognition (LPR)

When staff scans a plate at the lane:
- System looks up `vehicles WHERE plate = X`
- If found → known customer, apply subscription, log to history
- If not → create anonymous vehicle record, KedaiPOS captures the sale
- When this customer eventually signs up online, their plate matches → all historical wash data retroactively links to their account

**Result:** No customer is ever lost to "anonymous walk-in." Online and offline experiences are unified by the plate.

### Why the membership program is the keystone

The membership program is what turns walk-in customers into engaged online community:

```
Lane: "Want to save on every wash? $50/month unlimited."
   ↓
Staff: scans plate, captures phone, sends WhatsApp signup link
   ↓
Customer: clicks link → Google login (1 tap) → Pocket Pay → done
   ↓
Future visits: just drive in, system recognizes, no payment needed
```

This solves the chicken-and-egg problem: customers don't sign up online without a reason, and they don't have a reason until they see value. Lane staff push the value at the moment it's most relevant.

## 9. Operational principles (non-negotiable)

1. **KedaiPOS keeps running.** Lane revenue is sacred. Never make a change that risks branch operations.

2. **Backups before any DB change.** Run `pg_dump` and download to laptop. Three backups already on Hakem's laptop from May 2-3.

3. **Audit everything.** Every meaningful action logged in `audit_log`. (Table to be created Week 1.)

4. **Staging before production.** All schema changes test on staging Neon first.

5. **Localized for Brunei.**
   - Currency: BND with proper formatting
   - Phone: +673 country code
   - Plate format: configurable
   - Bahasa Melayu toggle (Month 4)
   - Hijri calendar option

6. **No vendor lock-in.** Auth self-hosted. DB portable Postgres. Code in our GitHub.

7. **Documentation is part of the build.** Every feature ships with doc updates. RUNBOOK.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, ADRs.

8. **Walk-ins AND online community equally.** Lane works for customers who never open the app. App works for customers who never wait at the lane.
