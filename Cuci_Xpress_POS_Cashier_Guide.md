# Cuci Xpress — Cashier Guide (POS System)

A simple, step-by-step handbook for every cashier at every branch.
Keep this near the till. If anything on screen does not match this guide, ask your manager.

---

## Golden rules (read first)

1. **Open your shift before your first sale.** No shift = the drawer is not counted.
2. **Always enter the car's plate number** for every order — it links the wash to the right customer and reward.
3. **Take the money first, then press Submit Order.** Once you submit, the ticket is issued.
4. **Hand the printed ticket (or tell the ticket code) to the lane** so they know which car to wash.
5. **Count and close your shift at the end of the day.** Never leave it open.
6. Use **Chrome or Edge** on an Android tablet, phone, or computer. The till does **not** work on iPhone/iPad.

---

## The sale screen at a glance

When you open the POS you will see, from top to bottom:

- **Top bar** — your name, your branch, and a coloured **shift pill** (Open shift / Shift open).
- **Step 1 — License Plate** — type the car's plate and identify the customer.
- **Step 2 — Package** — choose the wash.
- **Step 3 — Add-ons** — optional extras.
- **Payment** — choose how the customer is paying.
- **Order Summary** (right side) — the running total. Check this before you submit.
- **Today** (bottom) — the list of orders done today, with a **Refund** button on each.

---

## 1. Start of day — Open your shift

1. Look at the top bar. You will see an amber button: **"Open shift"**.
2. Click **"Open shift"**. A box titled **"Open shift"** appears.
3. In **"Opening float (BND)"**, type the cash you are starting the drawer with, e.g. `50.00`.
4. (Optional) Add a note in **"Note (optional)"**.
5. Click **"Open shift"**.
6. You will see **"Shift opened — Drawer ready."** The pill turns green: **"Shift open · Float B$50 · [time]"**.

> If you see **"You already have an open shift. Close it first."** — your previous shift was never closed. Close it (see Section 11), then open a new one.

---

## 2. Walk-in customer paying with CASH

This is the most common sale.

1. **Step 1 — License Plate:** type the plate, e.g. `BB1234`.
   - If the car has been here before, a suggestion list pops up — tap the right one. A **"Vehicle on file"** card appears with the car and the customer's name.
   - For a brand-new car, just leave the typed plate. You may add the **brand / model / colour** if you wish.
   - (Optional) Click **"+ Add customer info (optional)"** to type the customer's **Phone** and **Name**.
2. **Step 2 — Package:** click the wash they want, e.g. **"Basic Wash"** or **"Full Package"**. The most chosen one shows a **"Most popular"** badge.
3. **Step 3 — Add-ons:** (optional) tap any extras, e.g. **"Tyre Shine"**, **"Water Repellent"**.
4. Check the **Order Summary** on the right — the **Total** must be correct.
5. **Payment:** open the **Payment Method** dropdown and choose **"Cash"**.
6. An **"Amount received"** box appears. Type how much cash the customer handed you.
   - The screen shows the **Change due** — give that change back.
7. Click **"Submit Order"**.
8. The **"Ticket issued"** screen shows a big **ticket code** (e.g. `A01`), the total, amount paid, and change.
9. Click **"Print receipt"** if the customer wants one. Hand the ticket to the lane.
10. Click **"New order"** to serve the next customer.

---

## 3. Walk-in paying with CARD

Same as Section 2, but at **Payment**:

1. Choose **"Card"** in the dropdown.
2. Run the card on your card machine as usual.
3. Once the machine approves, click **"Submit Order"**.
4. Print / hand over the ticket, then **"New order"**.

---

## 4. Paying with an E-WALLET / QR (Pocket Pay, etc.)

Use this when the customer pays by scanning a QR or via an online payment app.

1. Do Steps 1–3 (plate, package, add-ons) as normal.
2. At **Payment Method**, choose the matching option:
   - **"Pocket Payment QR"** — customer scans the Pocket Pay QR.
   - **"Pocket Payment Invoice"** — Pocket Pay invoice/link.
   - **"Baiduri MS Payment Request"** — Baiduri payment request.
   - **"Baiduripay"** or **"Quickpay"** — those wallet apps.
3. A **"Reference"** box appears. Type the payment reference (e.g. **last 4 digits / transaction id**) once the customer's payment shows as successful.
4. Click **"Submit Order"** only after the money is confirmed received.
5. Print / hand over the ticket, then **"New order"**.

---

## 5. Paying by BANK TRANSFER

1. Do Steps 1–3 as normal.
2. At **Payment Method**, choose **"Bank Transfer"**.
3. In the **"Reference"** box, type the transfer reference (last 4 digits / txn id).
4. Confirm the money arrived, then click **"Submit Order"**.

---

## 6. Returning customer (plate already on file)

1. In **Step 1**, start typing the plate. Pick the car from the suggestion list.
2. A **"Vehicle on file"** card shows the car details and the customer's name.
3. You may also see a VIP badge — **"★ GOLD VIP"**, **"★ SILVER VIP"**, or **"★ BRONZE VIP"** with their rank (e.g. `#1`). Treat VIPs warmly.
4. Continue with package, add-ons, and payment exactly like a normal sale.

---

## 7. Customer with a PREPAID WASH PACK

Some customers have already paid for a bundle of washes in advance.

1. Enter the plate / pick the customer in **Step 1**.
2. If they have a pack, you'll see a hint: **"Active wash pack found"**.
3. Choose the **Package** they are using as normal.
4. At **Payment Method**, choose **"Subscription"**.
5. The **Order Summary** subtracts the wash with a **"Discount (Membership)"** line — the customer pays **B$0** (or only for extra add-ons not covered).
6. Click **"Submit Order"** and hand over the ticket.

> If you see **"No active wash pack found for this customer."**, the customer does **not** have a pack — take normal payment instead.

---

## 8. SUBSCRIPTION / UNLIMITED member (free wash)

For customers on the **Unlimited Wash** or **Multi-Car Family** monthly plan, washes are free during their plan.

1. Enter the plate / pick the customer in **Step 1**.
2. When the customer has an active **Unlimited** plan, a badge shows **"Unlimited"** and a green button appears:
   - **"Free Unlimited wash · B$0"**
3. Click that green **"Free Unlimited wash · B$0"** button.
   - This is the quick path — you do **not** need to pick a package or payment method.
4. The ticket is issued at **B$0**. Hand it to the lane.

> If they also want a paid **add-on** that isn't part of the plan, do a normal order, choose **"Subscription"** as the payment method, and the plan still covers the wash while they pay for the extra.

---

## 9. LOYALTY reward — 4 × B$12 = FREE wash

Customers collect a stamp each time they buy a B$12 wash. After 4, they earn one free wash, which they redeem from their phone as a **QR code**.

1. The customer opens their app/dashboard and taps **"Redeem free wash"** — their phone shows a QR.
2. On the till, click the **"Scan QR"** button (top of the plate section).
3. The **"Scan-In · Prepaid or Free-wash QR"** box opens. Click **"Start camera"**.
4. Point the camera at the customer's QR.
5. When it reads successfully, you'll see **"Ticket allocated"** and a **"Prepaid · PAID"** status — the free wash is booked. No payment is taken.
6. Hand over / tell them the ticket code.

> **Camera won't start?** You'll see *"Could not start camera. Check camera permissions and try again."* Allow camera access in the browser, or use a different tablet.

---

## 10. Customer scans their own prepaid / free-wash QR

If a customer arrives with a QR (prepaid wash or a free-wash voucher):

1. Click **"Scan QR"** → **"Start camera"**.
2. Scan their QR. The system books the right ticket automatically (**"Ticket allocated"**).
3. No payment needed unless the screen says otherwise.

---

## 11. Extras you may need on any order

### Discount / promo code
- In the **Payment** area, there is a code box (placeholder **"e.g. CUCI10"**). Type the promo code the customer gives you; the total updates.

### Car colour / note
- There is a note box (placeholder **"e.g. silver Mini Cooper"**). Use it to help the lane spot the car.

### Reprint or share a receipt
- On the **"Ticket issued"** screen, click **"Print receipt"** to print, or **"Save"** to download it as an image you can send the customer.

---

## 12. Refunds

Only refund when a manager approves, or per your branch rules.

1. Scroll to the **"Today"** list at the bottom of the screen.
2. Find the order and click its **"Refund"** button.
3. A box titled **"Refund this order?"** opens.
4. (Optional) Type a **"Reason"** for the refund.
5. Click the red **"Refund"** button to confirm.
6. The order goes grey, the ticket code is crossed out, and a red **"Refunded"** badge appears.

> Cash refunds are taken **out of your drawer**, so they reduce your expected cash at close. Keep any paperwork.

---

## 13. End of day — Close your shift & count cash

1. Click the green pill at the top: **"Shift open · Float B$… · [time]"**.
2. The **"Close shift"** box opens. It shows:
   - **Net sales**, **Transaction fees (MDR)**, **Net after fees** (your sales summary).
   - A cash count:
     - **Opening float**
     - **+ Cash sales**
     - **− Cash refunds**
     - **Expected cash in drawer** (the amount you *should* have).
3. Count the physical cash in the drawer.
4. Type the counted amount in **"Counted cash (BND)"**.
5. The screen shows one of:
   - **"Balanced"** (green) — perfect.
   - **"Over by B$X"** — more cash than expected.
   - **"Short by B$X"** — less cash than expected.
6. Click **"Close shift"**.
7. You'll see **"Shift closed"** with the result (e.g. *Drawer balanced* or *Short by B$5.00*).

> If you're **short or over**, tell your manager and note why before closing.

## 14. Common messages & quick fixes

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **"Open shift"** (amber) | No shift is open | Open your shift before selling (Section 1) |
| **"You already have an open shift…"** | Old shift left open | Close the old shift, then open a new one |
| **"Vehicle on file"** | Plate is a returning car | Pick it; details fill in automatically |
| **"Active wash pack found"** | Customer has a prepaid pack | Use **Subscription** payment to apply it |
| **"Free Unlimited wash · B$0"** | Customer is on the Unlimited plan | Click it — wash is free, no payment |
| **"No active wash pack found…"** | No pack/plan on this customer | Take normal payment |
| **"Ticket allocated" / "Prepaid · PAID"** | A scanned QR was accepted | Hand over the ticket, no payment |
| **"Could not start camera…"** | Camera blocked or unsupported | Allow camera access / use Android Chrome/Edge |
| **"Ticket collision — please try again."** | Two tickets clashed | Just submit again |

---

## 15. Quick cheat-sheet

- **Normal sale:** Plate → Package → (Add-ons) → Payment Method → (Amount received / Reference) → **Submit Order** → Print → **New order**.
- **Unlimited member:** Plate → green **"Free Unlimited wash · B$0"** → done.
- **Prepaid pack:** Plate → Package → Payment = **"Subscription"** → **Submit Order**.
- **Free loyalty wash:** **"Scan QR"** → **"Start camera"** → scan customer's QR → done.
- **Refund:** **Today** list → **"Refund"** → reason → confirm.
- **Open day:** amber **"Open shift"** → float → **Open shift**.
- **Close day:** green pill → count cash → **"Counted cash (BND)"** → **Close shift**.
