---
name: Branch shell accounts hold bulk-claimed plates
description: Why customers report "this plate is mine" and how to resolve it
---

Branch staff Gmail accounts (e.g. the Tutong branch login) bulk-claimed roughly 100 walk-in cars each, so those plates are "taken" and the real customer cannot add their car — the unique plate constraint blocks them. Expect recurring "this vehicle plate is mine" WhatsApp complaints.

**Why:** Early on, branch accounts were used to register walk-in cars at the counter, which set the car's owner to the branch account instead of leaving it unclaimed.

**How to apply:** Use the owner-only Plate Ownership Transfer tool in the admin Customers tab (lookup by plate → transfer to the right customer, or detach to unclaimed). Wash history and loyalty follow the car (orders are keyed by vehicle, not account), so transferring is safe and preserves history.
