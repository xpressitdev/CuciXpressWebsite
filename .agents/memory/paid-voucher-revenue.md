---
name: Paid voucher revenue timing
description: Owner-directed revenue recognition for purchased physical wash vouchers.
---

Record the entire paid voucher sale as revenue on its original sale date, in the month sold. Do not treat it as money received in advance.

**Why:** The owner explicitly instructed: "should appear instantly, not received in advance. meaning the whole sales should be captured as revenue for the month".

**How to apply:** Voucher-sale reporting and historical backfills must use the sale date. Redemption records the wash without recognising the same revenue again. Keep purchased vouchers distinct from free loyalty rewards.
