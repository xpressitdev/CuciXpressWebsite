---
name: Paid voucher revenue timing
description: Owner-directed revenue recognition for purchased physical wash vouchers.
---

Record the entire paid voucher sale as revenue on its original sale date, in the month sold. Do not treat it as money received in advance.

**Why:** The owner explicitly instructed: "should appear instantly, not received in advance. meaning the whole sales should be captured as revenue for the month".

**How to apply:** Voucher-sale reporting and historical backfills must use the sale date. Redemption records the wash without recognising the same revenue again. Keep purchased vouchers distinct from free loyalty rewards.

Do not guess a branch or payment method for historical offline sales, or split unassigned sales equally across branches.

**Why:** The owner supplied historical voucher sales without branch/payment details. Unlike Connecteam expenses explicitly tagged All, unknown sales attribution is not an instruction to allocate across branches or alter a cash drawer.

**How to apply:** Keep unknown attribution explicit and include these sales only in Overall P&L until the owner supplies a branch. Backfills must not create wash tickets or change today's drawer.

Do not globally zero every order paid with the voucher payment method.

**Why:** The business already has multiple voucher programs, zero-priced redemption packages, and legacy nonzero voucher transactions. A payment-method-wide change can unintentionally alter unrelated programs or paid extras.

**How to apply:** Use the existing zero-priced redemption package for these B$9 physical wash vouchers. Treat serial-level issuance/redemption reconciliation as a separate feature, not an assumption that all historical voucher orders match this sales register.
