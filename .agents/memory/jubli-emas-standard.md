---
name: Jubli Emas standardisation
description: JE naming and the deliberate no-merge boundary for existing vehicle identities.
---

New Jubli Emas entries should use JE followed by the number without spaces. Recognise the long spelling in lookups, but do not automatically merge or rename existing vehicle records.

**Why:** The owner approved standardising new entries and preparing existing duplicates for review, explicitly without automatic merges. Matching digits alone do not establish ownership or entitlement; existing records can have separate histories and memberships.

**How to apply:** Reuse a unique existing identity without changing its stored plate. If both spellings identify multiple vehicles, require owner review rather than choosing arbitrarily. Any future consolidation requires separate approval and must preserve vehicle-linked benefits and history.
